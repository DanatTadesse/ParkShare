Frequently Asked Questions

What is ParkShare?

ParkShare is a platform concept that connects drivers with people who have available parking spaces.

Can I share my parking space?

Yes. The platform is designed to allow parking space owners to list their available spaces.

Are reservations confirmed automatically?

No. This student project currently demonstrates a reservation request form only.
