About ParkShare

Making parking easier through sharing.

Who We Are

ParkShare is a proposed online platform designed to connect drivers searching for parking spaces with property owners who have available parking areas.

The idea is to make better use of unused parking spaces while helping drivers find convenient places to park their vehicles.

Our Mission

To make parking more accessible and convenient by connecting parking space owners with drivers.

Our Vision

To become a trusted parking space sharing platform that supports smarter urban mobility.

Our Objectives

  1. Help drivers find available parking spaces.
  2. Reduce the time spent searching for parking.
  3. Help owners earn income from unused spaces.
  4. Encourage efficient use of parking areas.
  5. Provide clear parking location and price information.

Benefits of ParkShare

Benefits for Drivers

  • More parking choices.
  • Ability to compare sample prices.
  • Convenient reservation requests.

Benefits for Parking Owners

  • Opportunity to earn extra income.
  • Better use of unused parking spaces.
  • Connection with potential customers.
