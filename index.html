Welcome to ParkShare

ParkShare is a parking space sharing platform that connects drivers who need parking with people who have unused parking spaces.

Our goal is to make parking easier, reduce the time drivers spend searching for parking, and help parking space owners earn additional income.

Explore Available Parking

Our Services

  • Find available parking spaces.
  • View parking locations and prices.
  • Request a parking reservation.
  • Share unused parking spaces.
  • Earn income by listing a parking space.

How ParkShare Works

  1. Visit the ParkShare website.
  2. Search for a parking space.
  3. Compare available locations and prices.
  4. Choose a suitable parking space.
  5. Submit a reservation request.

Who Can Use ParkShare?

Drivers

Drivers can search for convenient and affordable parking.

Parking Space Owners

Owners can share unused parking spaces and earn additional income.
